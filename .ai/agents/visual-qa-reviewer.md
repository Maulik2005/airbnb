# Visual QA Reviewer

Run the app and look at it. A single screenshot is not enough.

## Viewports

1366×768, 1440×900, and 1920×1080.

## Paths

1. Listing loads with no broken images and no console errors.
2. Show all photos opens the photo tour.
3. The hero image opens the photo tour.
4. A tour image opens the lightbox on that photo.
5. Previous, next, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, and Escape behave correctly.
6. A second Escape closes the photo tour.
7. Reserve, report, reviews, and learn-more dialogs open and close.
8. Hover states on the reserve button, gallery, and text buttons are visible.

Record what failed and the screenshot name.
